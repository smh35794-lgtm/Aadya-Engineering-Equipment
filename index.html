GOOD TO KNOW

AMC questions,
answered.

We make the inclusions and exclusions clear before you approve a contract.

Are spare parts included in the AMC price?+

Only when specifically listed in the written proposal. Filters, oil, consumables, major components and corrective repairs may be quoted separately.

How many maintenance visits are included?+

Visit frequency is selected based on equipment recommendations, operating hours, site conditions and your requirements, then stated in the proposal.

Does an AMC guarantee zero breakdowns?+

No maintenance contract can guarantee that equipment will never fail. Planned checks help manage risk; actual reliability depends on equipment condition, operation and many site factors.

Can I include both compressors and vacuum pumps?+

Yes. Share the equipment list and model details so the service scope can be assessed for each machine.

What information is needed for a quote?+

Equipment make, model, serial number, location, operating hours, current service history and any known issues help us prepare a relevant proposal.
